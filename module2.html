Module 2: Classroom Environment & Daily Management

Create a calm, predictable, and accessible classroom that reduces anxiety and helps all students focus on learning.

[Embedded Video Player: "The Power of a Structured Classroom" - 2 min]

Key Takeaways:

  • Predictability reduces anxiety. Visual schedules and clear routines help students know what to expect.
  • Structure promotes independence. Well-defined areas and organized materials help students navigate their day.
  • A sensory-friendly classroom benefits everyone. Reducing clutter and noise helps all students concentrate better.
  • These strategies are low-cost and high-impact. You can implement most of them with paper, markers, and thoughtful planning.
馃挕 UDL Connection: A well-designed environment provides multiple means of engagement by reducing threats and distractions. It creates a safe space where all learners can thrive.

鈴?Routines and Transitions UDL

Why it works: Clear routines save instructional time and reduce student anxiety about what comes next. Smooth transitions are key to maintaining a calm classroom flow.

Essential Routines to Teach:

Morning Entry
Where to put bags, first activity
Handing in Work
Clear procedure and location
Lining Up
Consistent expectations
Emergency Drills
Practice with visual supports

Transition Strategies:

  • Teach Routines Explicitly: Don't assume students know them. Model and practice repeatedly.
  • Use Transition Warnings: Give 5-minute and 1-minute warnings. Use visual timers.
  • Implement Transition Cues: Consistent song, chime, or hand signal.
  • Visual Instructions: Numbered visual lists for multi-step tasks (packing bags, washing hands).
In a Kazakhstani Classroom: These strategies are especially helpful in multi-grade or large classes where management is crucial. A consistent transition song can work wonders.
